\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Current agent evaluation remains fragmented across \emph{benchmark islands}, and the very target of evaluation---``trustworthiness''---is rarely defined in operational terms. We give a deployment-oriented definition through five measurable properties---Reliability, Robustness, Safety, Social-Ethical Alignment, and Operational Integrity (P1--P5)---and introduce HAAF, which combines distribution-aware sampling with multi-layer probing and an iterative \emph{Trustworthy Optimization Factory} aligned to these properties. Our instantiation shows three results in concert: a 24-scenario Factory cycle narrows the focal model's vulnerability surface from 16.7\% to 4.2\% with no new improper refusals; a 100-scenario cross-model profile over 13 systems from seven families surfaces property-level trade-offs no scalar leaderboard captures, including a near-universal P3 (Safety) and P4 (Social-Ethical) weakness under user-instructed violations and an \emph{anti-scaling} pattern across four intra-family pairs (three significant at $p<0.05$ under a paired-scenario bootstrap with $B=10{,}000$; per-axis $n{=}14$--$26$ keeps per-cell CIs at roughly $\pm 0.2$, so these are cross-family-trend descriptors, not powered per-property claims); and the focal-model interventions, applied uniformly to all 13 systems, reduce $\mathrm{RWF}$ in every case (two to $\mathrm{RWF}=0.000$, with a marginal $\Delta{=}0.019$ on Qwen3-32B), and one intervention-resistant case (Qwen3-32B) is flagged for a fundamentally different second Factory cycle---direct evidence that property-targeted hardening transfers across model families while preserving visibility into where it does not.

\paragraph{Contributions, restated.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,label=(C\arabic*)]
\item \textbf{Definition.} A five-property operational definition of agentic trustworthiness (P1--P5) with Improper Refusal as a first-class taxonomy class mapped to P1.
\item \textbf{Cross-model evaluation.} 13 models (spanning 7 families) $\times$ 2 versions (Control/Treated) $\times$ 100 scenarios = $2{,}600$ trajectories on a shared sandbox.
\item \textbf{End-to-end instantiation.} Red-team property-level attribution, blue-team targeted interventions, and re-evaluation on the same sandbox, demonstrating cross-family transfer.
\item \textbf{Layer-4 reference implementation.} A 40-scenario subset selected by the released sampling objective preserves the cross-model ranking against the full suite (Kendall-$\tau$\,=\,0.890, Spearman-$\rho$\,=\,0.963).
\item \textbf{Statistical confidence.} Wilson 95\%~CIs on per-property success rates and paired-scenario bootstrap (B\,=\,10{,}000) on four intra-family anti-scaling pairs; three reject the larger-is-no-worse null at $p<0.05$.
\item \textbf{Explicit scope.} Hand-authored scenarios and sandbox tools, single LLM-judge for IR validation (Kimi-K2.5, itself one of the 13 evaluated agents), and no production-trace grounding---honestly bounded and addressed in v2.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{v2 commitments.}
We commit four concrete next steps as HAAF~v2. (i)~\emph{Deployment-grounded sampler}: replace hand-authored Layer-4 weights with weights derived from operation-class frequencies in publicly released production traces, so the sampling objective is parameterised by real telemetry rather than designer priors. (ii)~\emph{ToolSandbox Level-2 fidelity}: lift the current hand-authored sandbox to ToolSandbox L2 (stateful, multi-tool, side-effecting) so trajectories reflect realistic tool-chain dynamics rather than single-step calls. (iii)~\emph{Multi-judge IR gold}: replace the single LLM-judge for Improper-Refusal adjudication with a multi-judge ensemble plus a human-annotated gold subset, and report inter-rater agreement. (iv)~\emph{Broader model coverage}: extend beyond the present 7 families and 13 models to include additional open-weight releases, proprietary frontier models accessed via API, and longitudinal versioned evaluations of the same family over time.

Without representative, property-aware evaluation, benchmark progress will remain only weakly connected to deployment trustworthiness; we hope this work motivates a broader agenda around scenario modelling, risk-aware sampling, and iterative deployment-readiness assessment for agentic information-retrieval systems and beyond. The full 100-scenario validation suite, the seven-family Bedrock adapter, and all $2{,}600$ Control/Treated trajectories are released at \url{https://github.com/TonyQJH/haaf-pilot} to support replication, scenario extension, and second-cycle Factory studies on intervention-resistant systems such as Qwen3-32B.
