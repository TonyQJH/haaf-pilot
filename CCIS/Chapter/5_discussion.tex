


\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Implications for agentic information retrieval.}
\emph{Retrieval-grounded action}~\cite{xi2024agentir} makes agentic IR a high-stakes setting for this framework. Retrieved content is an attack surface (indirect prompt injection~\cite{greshake2023indirect,zhou2024poisonedrag} is a P2 failure), and natural-language access to sensitive corpora makes P3 (Safety) and P4 (Social-Ethical) joint rather than separable. A system top-ranked on retrieval helpfulness may rank last on injection resistance: only profile-level comparison reveals which trade-off matches a given deployment.
