\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Agent evaluation remains fragmented across \emph{benchmark islands}, and ``trustworthiness'' is rarely defined in operational terms. We give a five-property operational definition---Reliability, Robustness, Safety, Social-Ethical Alignment, Operational Integrity (P1--P5)---and instantiate HAAF, which combines distribution-aware sampling with multi-layer probing and an iterative \emph{Trustworthy Optimization Factory}. A 24-scenario Factory cycle narrows the focal model's vulnerability surface from 16.7\% to 4.2\% with no new improper refusals; a 100-scenario profile over 13 models from seven families surfaces property-level trade-offs no scalar leaderboard captures, including near-universal P3/P4 weakness under user-instructed violations and an \emph{anti-scaling} pattern across four intra-family pairs (two reject at $p<0.05$, a third at the $p=0.050$ boundary, $B{=}10{,}000$; per-axis $n{=}14$--$26$ keeps per-cell CIs near $\pm 0.2$). Focal-model interventions, applied uniformly to all 13 systems, reduce $\mathrm{RWF}$ in every case, with one intervention-resistant outlier (Qwen3-32B) flagged for a second cycle.

\paragraph{Implications for agentic information retrieval.}
\emph{Retrieval-grounded action}~\cite{xi2024agentir} makes agentic IR a high-stakes setting for this framework. Retrieved content is an attack surface (indirect prompt injection~\cite{greshake2023indirect,zhou2024poisonedrag} is a P2 failure), and natural-language access to sensitive corpora makes P3 (Safety) and P4 (Social-Ethical) joint rather than separable. A system top-ranked on retrieval helpfulness may rank last on injection resistance: only profile-level comparison reveals which trade-off matches a given deployment.

\paragraph{Contributions, restated.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,label=(C\arabic*)]
\item \textbf{Definition.} Five-property operational trustworthiness (P1--P5) with Improper Refusal as a first-class class mapped to P1.
\item \textbf{Cross-model evaluation.} 13 models, 7 families, 2 versions, 100 scenarios = $2{,}600$ trajectories on a shared sandbox.
\item \textbf{End-to-end instantiation.} Red-team attribution, blue-team interventions, and re-evaluation showing cross-family transfer.
\item \textbf{Layer-4 reference implementation.} 40-of-100 subset preserves the cross-model ranking (Kendall-$\tau$\,=\,0.890, Spearman-$\rho$\,=\,0.963).
\item \textbf{Statistical confidence.} Wilson 95\%~CIs per property and paired-scenario bootstrap ($B{=}10{,}000$) on four anti-scaling pairs; two reject larger-is-no-worse at $p<0.05$, with a third at the $p=0.050$ boundary.
\item \textbf{Explicit scope.} Hand-authored scenarios, single LLM-judge (Kimi-K2.5, itself one of the 13 agents), no production-trace grounding---bounded and addressed in v2.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{v2 commitments.}
HAAF~v2 will: (i)~replace hand-authored Layer-4 weights with a \emph{deployment-grounded sampler} parameterised by operation-class frequencies from public production traces; (ii)~lift the sandbox to \emph{ToolSandbox Level-2 fidelity} (stateful, multi-tool, side-effecting); (iii)~replace the single LLM-judge with a \emph{multi-judge IR gold} ensemble plus a human-annotated subset reporting inter-rater agreement; and (iv)~extend \emph{model coverage} beyond 7 families and 13 models to additional open-weight releases, API-accessed proprietary frontier models, and longitudinal versioned evaluations.

The full 100-scenario suite, seven-family Bedrock adapter, and all $2{,}600$ Control/Treated trajectories are released at \url{https://github.com/TonyQJH/haaf-pilot}.
